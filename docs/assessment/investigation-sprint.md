# Practical exercise

Use a synthetic or approved redacted scenario.

## Timeboxed workflow

- **10 minutes:** define one IR, priority questions and SIRs.
- **15 minutes:** collect from approved public sources only.
- **15 minutes:** document leads and classify sensitivity.
- **10 minutes:** validate two findings and assign confidence.
- **10 minutes:** produce a three-row findings matrix.

## Submission requirements

Submit the tasking statement, collection plan, investigation log, findings matrix, caveats and recommended handling. Do not submit raw credentials, personal addresses, phone numbers or unredacted identifiers.

## Assessment criteria

- Scope and requirement quality
- Legal and ethical compliance
- Evidential traceability
- Verification discipline
- Sensitivity handling
- Clear, actionable judgement

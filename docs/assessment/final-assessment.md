# Release-readiness assessment

Complete the following before a finding is released:

- [ ] Tasking and SIRs are explicit.
- [ ] Collection authority and boundaries are recorded.
- [ ] Sources, URLs and timestamps are traceable.
- [ ] Significant claims are independently verified.
- [ ] Sensitive identifiers are redacted.
- [ ] Confidence and caveats are assigned.
- [ ] Legal or privacy review is complete where required.
- [ ] An action owner is identified.

## Final knowledge check

1. Why is a username match not identity proof?
2. What separates a lead from a finding?
3. When must collection stop and escalate?
4. Why can aggregation increase sensitivity?
5. What information must every investigation-log entry contain?

## Completion standard

A successful investigation answers the tasking question safely and defensibly. The core risk may not be the original compromise; it may be the persistent, structured availability of identity-linked data that enables future targeting.

# A report must be traceable and restrained

A defensible report contains:

1. Tasking, IRs, PIRs and SIRs
2. Collection authority, boundaries and source classes
3. Findings matrix
4. Sensitivity and risk assessment
5. Caveats, intelligence gaps and confidence
6. Recommended handling and next actions

## Findings matrix

| SIR | Finding | Sensitivity | Confidence | Handling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Public-source references to alleged material remain discoverable | Moderate | Medium | Redacted summary |
| 2 | Observed artefacts are primarily personal or PII | Moderate | Medium | Mask identifiers |
| 3 | Aggregated personal context may increase targeting risk | High | Medium | Restricted distribution |

The report should answer what was asked, explain how the judgement was formed and avoid unnecessary republication of sensitive material.

# Confidence is earned

Confidence depends on relevance, independence and source quality.

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Lead | A single artefact or unverified claim |
| Finding | A relevant claim verified through an independent source class |
| Assessment | Multiple findings support an analytic judgement |

Use calibrated language. Do not convert tool output, visual similarity or username reuse into identity proof.

## Analyst checks

- Are sources genuinely independent?
- Is the artefact relevant to a stated SIR?
- Is chronology consistent?
- Could an alternative explanation fit the evidence?
- What would change the judgement?
- Are confidence and caveats explicit?

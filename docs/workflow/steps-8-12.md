# Turn artefacts into defensible findings

## 8. Personal and professional web
Review public sites, blogs, official profiles and archives for relevant context.

## 9. Digital-footprint mapping
Map observed identities, platforms, timing and relationships. Distinguish observation from inference.

## 10. Document and analyse
Record source, URL, timestamp, artefact, relevance, sensitivity, source quality and confidence.

## 11. Verify and validate
Cross-reference significant findings across independent source classes. One source is a lead; corroborated evidence can become a finding.

## 12. Consult experts
Escalate legal uncertainty, jurisdictional risk, protected information, language gaps, specialist technical artefacts and high-impact reporting decisions.

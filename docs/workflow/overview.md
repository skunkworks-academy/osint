---

# Repeatable operating model

1. Define scope
2. Conduct initial research
3. Tailor queries
4. Use approved platforms
5. Capture timestamps and metadata
6. Perform authorised real-time collection
7. Assess breach exposure safely
8. Review public websites and blogs
9. Map the digital footprint
10. Document and analyse
11. Verify and validate
12. Consult experts

The workflow moves from tasking to reporting while preserving authority, traceability, proportionality and evidence quality.

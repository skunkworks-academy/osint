# Intelligence requirements control the investigation

## IR — Intelligence Requirement
The complete set of questions the investigation must answer.

## PIR — Priority Intelligence Requirement
The critical questions that receive resources and attention first.

## SIR — Specific Intelligence Requirement
Granular facts, indicators or artefacts required to answer an IR or PIR.

## Practice

Transform this broad request into structured requirements:

> Assess the public-source risk associated with an alleged account compromise.

Create one IR, two PIRs and three SIRs. For each SIR, define the relevance threshold, permitted source classes and reporting threshold.

# Where OSINT lives

OSINT contributes to every stage of the intelligence cycle:

1. **Planning:** define requirements, authority, scope and decision needs.
2. **Collection:** acquire relevant public-source data.
3. **Exploitation:** make collected data usable through extraction, normalization and chronology.
4. **Analysis:** evaluate reliability, correlate sources and form judgements.
5. **Sharing:** communicate findings, caveats, confidence and required action.

Requirements drive collection. Collection creates data. Exploitation makes data usable. Analysis creates judgement. Sharing turns judgement into action.

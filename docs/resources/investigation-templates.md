## Analytical summary structure

1. **Requirement:** State the question answered.
2. **Key judgement:** Provide the principal assessment in one or two sentences.
3. **Confidence:** State high, medium or low confidence and explain the basis.
4. **Supporting findings:** Present the strongest corroborated findings.
5. **Contradicting evidence:** Identify material that weakens the assessment.
6. **Gaps and limitations:** State what could not be verified.
7. **Implications:** Explain why the finding matters to the decision-maker.
8. **Recommended next steps:** List lawful, proportionate follow-up actions.

## Pre-submission quality control

- Every finding maps to an intelligence requirement.
- Every material assertion has a source or is clearly marked as assessment.
- Source independence has been checked.
- Dates, names, URLs and identifiers have been normalised.
- Sensitive information has been minimised and handled correctly.
- Alternative explanations are recorded.
- Confidence language matches the available evidence.
- The report distinguishes fact, inference and unresolved uncertainty.

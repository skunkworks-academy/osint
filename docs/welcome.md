# Finding what is already there

This self-paced course moves you from ad-hoc searching to structured intelligence work.

## Outcomes

By the end, you will be able to:

- distinguish data, information and intelligence;
- apply the 12-step OSINT workflow from tasking to reporting;
- identify ethical and legal boundaries for open-source collection;
- evaluate findings to a confidence and evidential standard; and
- deliver a defensible investigation report.

## Course standard

**Methodology first. Legal by design. Evidence-ready.**

One source is a lead. Verified findings become intelligence. The objective is not to find everything; it is to answer the intelligence requirement safely and defensibly.

:::warning Public-source boundary
This course does not authorise hacking, credential testing, access-control bypass, harassment, doxxing or collection behind login walls.
:::

## Learning path

1. Foundations and intelligence requirements
2. The 12-step workflow
3. Controlled case study
4. Correlation, confidence and reporting
5. Investigation sprint and final assessment

# From baseline to validated findings

## Steps 1–3
Define SIRs, public-source boundaries, redaction requirements and reporting thresholds. Establish a baseline using official biographies, credible reporting, public statements and authoritative records. Build queries from names, dates, role titles and incident labels without exposing sensitive identifiers.

## Steps 4–7
Use approved search, reverse-media and exposure tools. Capture timestamps and metadata to build a chronology. Monitor public corrections or new claims during the authorised window. Never test credentials.

## Steps 8–11
Review official profiles, public records and archived pages. Map observed identities, platforms and timing. Log every artefact. Validate important claims through independent sources.

## Step 12
Escalate unresolved legal, technical, linguistic or sensitivity questions before reporting.

### Baseline discipline

| Status | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Confirmed/public | Record authoritative source and time |
| Claimed | Mark for independent verification |
| Unknown | Do not infer or present as fact |

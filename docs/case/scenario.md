# Public claim of account compromise

A hacktivist group publicly claims it compromised a personal email account linked to a senior law-enforcement leader and published breach-derived material. The material is alleged to include emails, photographs and profile documents predating the person’s current office.

## Intelligence request

Assess the nature and sensitivity of breach-derived material that remains accessible on the open internet, and identify material that creates ongoing risk.

## SIRs

1. Catalogue and characterise material that remains openly accessible.
2. Assess sensitivity: personal/PII, business, operational or government-related context.
3. Identify personal material that becomes sensitive because of the subject’s current public role.

## Prohibited actions

No credential validation, contact attempts, harassment, scraping behind login walls or unnecessary exposure of personal data.
